Our Legacy

Rooted in Downtown Srinagar

Founded by Habibullah Hakak, educated at Islamia School Gojwara, House of Hakak began with a vision to share the unmatched beauty of Kashmiri craftsmanship. From a modest start in New Delhi, the family-led house has grown to serve over 10,000 homes across four countries, carrying forward three generations of heritage and standard.
