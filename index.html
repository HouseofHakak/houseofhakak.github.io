Atelier Service 02

Restoration & Organic Wash Spa

Pure cashmere and Pashmina require gentle organic care. Standard dry cleaners use harsh synthetic solvents that strip delicate cashmere fibers of natural lanolin oils.

Our traditional Srinagar spa cleanses and restores delicate heritage shawls using pure mountain water, organic reetha soaps, and expert darning artisans.

Do you own vintage Kashmiri Jamawar, antique Kani, or heirloom needlework shawls passed down through generations? House of Hakak evaluates vintage textiles for weave age, craft purity, and historical value.

Three Generations of Pure Craft
