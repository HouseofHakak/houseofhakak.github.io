Atelier Service 02

Restoration & Organic Wash Spa

Pashmina requires delicate organic care. Standard dry cleaners use harsh chemicals that strip cashmere of its natural lanolin oil. Our traditional Srinagar spa wash cleanses and rejuvenates your delicate heirloom shawls using pure mountain water and organic nut soaps.
