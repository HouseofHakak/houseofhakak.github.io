Our Story

A name first spoken in 1960

Habibulal Hakak began this house in 1960, on the belief that Kashmir's finest work — hand-spun pashm, hand-embroidered wool, and hand-picked saffron — deserved to be carried, not just sold.

What started as a single trade has grown into two: HK Shawls, devoted to pashmina, wool tailoring and Kashmiri art, and our saffron trade, sourced from the same soil that has always given this family its living. Today, the house is carried forward by the third generation, keeping the same standard Habibulal Hakak set at the start.
