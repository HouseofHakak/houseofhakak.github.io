Heritage & Lineage

Three Generations of Pure Craft

Founded in 1960 by Habibullah Hakak, House of Hakak was built on vision, grit, and an unyielding commitment to Kashmiri heritage. Born in Downtown Srinagar and educated at Islamia School Gojwara, Habibullah Hakak set out to New Delhi without family backing or financial leverage, starting our trade on a micro scale.

Through sheer perseverance, that humble beginning expanded into HK Shawls and Kashmir Foods—touching over 10,000 households globally. Today, spanning three generations, we remain dedicated to uncompromised pure handlooms, master Sozni needlework, and certified organic saffron.
