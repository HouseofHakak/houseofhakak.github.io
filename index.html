Founder
Three Generations

Rooted in Downtown Srinagar

Founded by Habibullah Hakak, educated at Islamia School Gojwara, House of Hakak began with an unyielding dedication to true Kashmiri craftsmanship.

What started as a micro-scale effort in New Delhi under HK Shawls has grown into a royal house serving over 10,000 homes across four countries—built entirely on trust, handloom purity, and authentic saffron.
