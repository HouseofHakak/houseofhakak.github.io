Heritage & Artisan Craft

House of Hakak

Rooted in Downtown Srinagar, House of Hakak brings together centuries of valley craft and heritage. Through HK Shawls, we preserve fine textile artistry—offering pure hand-loomed Pashmina, delicate Sozni needlework, and heritage Kani weaves.

Our legacy also celebrates Kashmir’s finest culinary treasures through Kashmir Foods—delivering authentic Pamposh Mongra Saffron, premium walnuts, and organic valley produce directly to you.
