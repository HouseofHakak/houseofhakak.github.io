Our Legacy

Rooted in Downtown Srinagar

Founded by Habibullah Hakak, educated at Islamia School Gojwara, House of Hakak began with a passion for true Kashmiri artistry. Built from a single micro-scale start in New Delhi into HK Shawls, our house now reaches over 10,000 homes across 4+ countries, carrying three generations of integrity and craft.
