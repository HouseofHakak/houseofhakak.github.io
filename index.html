Our Story

Born in downtown Srinagar

HABIBULAL HAKAK

Habibulal Hakak was born in the valley of Kashmir, in downtown Srinagar. He studied at Islamia School, Gojwara, up to the 10th standard — with no family business behind him, and no money to fall back on.

Wanting to build something of his own, he left home for New Delhi and began selling his goods on the smallest possible scale. What he started alone, with nothing to lean on, has grown into HK Shawls — now reaching over 10,000 homes across 4+ countries, alongside the family's saffron trade.
