Three-Generation Heritage

House of Hakak

A Luxury of Its Own Since 1960

Founded in 1960 by Habibullah Hakak, the story of House of Hakak is one of resilience, vision, and unyielding dedication to Kashmiri craftsmanship. Born in downtown Srinagar and educated at Islamia School Gojwara, Habibullah Hakak took a bold step forward—moving to New Delhi without family backing to establish our trade on a micro scale.

Through sheer perseverance, that micro endeavor evolved into HK Shawls, a name now cherished across more than 10,000 homes in four countries. Through HK Shawls, we continue to preserve Kashmir’s grandest textile traditions: pure hand-loomed Pashmina, delicate Sozni needlework, and heritage Kani weaves crafted by master artisans.

Spanning three generations, our family legacy also honors the pure culinary treasures of the valley through Kashmir Foods—delivering authentic Mongra Saffron, organic dry fruits, and traditional spices directly from Srinagar to the world.
